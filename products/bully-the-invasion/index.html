Bully: the Invasion

Bully is back! A new dark lord rises above the land, commanding evil soldiers, animated gears and waves of undead... but who is the mastermind behind this invasion? Only Bully can uncover the truth!

Features

This new chapter features thirty levels across five worlds — from mountains and deserts to urban streets and ancient tombs. You'll wield powerful weapons, carefully manage your ammo, and hunt for every coin to reach 100% completion. The soundtrack, a loving tribute to classic retro games, will warm the heart of every old-school gamer.
