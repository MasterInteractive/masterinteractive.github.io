Bully: the Legend

Bully is back! After the defeat of the evil Big Roll, a new enemy menaces the world from deep space: Big Gear! The fate of the world rests once again on Bully's shoulders — are you ready for the challenge?

Features

This new chapter features twenty levels over four worlds — from caves and mountains to ancient castles. Face brand new enemies like soldiers, ghosts and undead, and unleash new super-powers for Bully! The graphics are greatly improved from the previous chapter, and the soundtrack is a mix of classic retro games.
