The Yellow Shadow

In the misty Wiltshire, dark forces are at work. A series of mysterious events, disappearances, and murders will lead investigator Nathan Graves back to his hometown, following the trail of his family’s legacy. Are you ready to delve into the mystery?

Features

As investigator Nathan Graves, you’ll be drawn to Enday, a small village in Wiltshire, by a mysterious telegram. Despite the villagers’ secrecy and the growing number of disturbing events—abductions and strange journeys through dreams—you’ll need to use wit and imagination to piece together clues and unravel the mystery. But remember! Your fate lies in your own hands!
