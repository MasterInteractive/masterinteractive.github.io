The Elder Scrolls III: Mzanchtung

Under the deepest reaches of Morrowind, treasures await brave adventurers. Are you skilled enough to find and claim them?

Features

This mod will take you into a deep cavern where a forgotten dwemer ruin lies, filled with deadly automata and cunning traps. The dungeon will test your skills, but those brave enough to overcome it will be rewarded with unique equipment found nowhere else in Morrowind. To reach the cave entrance, head north from Balmora following the Odai river.
